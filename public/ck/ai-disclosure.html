AI Usage

This project was an exploration in how AI tools can be used to conduct math research and what communication of math research might look like in the future.

AI tools were used extensively throughout this project. GPT 5.6 Sol was used to discover the new proof argument for the Courtade–Kumar conjecture presented here, and GPT 6 Astra was used to write the Lean formalization and optimize the proof so that its Lean formalization would only take a few hours to compile on a MacBook Pro. The authors used GPT 5.6 Sol, GPT 6 Astra, and GPT 6.1 Sol to digest the contents of the proof, to understand the Lean certificates, and as a writing aid. We used Astra and Sol in the creation of the visual proof. Our visual proof was developed using Armstrong, Kuusi, and Loher’s visual companion to Homogenization at a polynomial scale in high contrast as a template, drawing extensively on its layout, navigation, and presentation of proof dependencies. We also used Astra and Sol to help draft the visual proof and the explanation of the Lean certificates before cleaning up each writeup by hand.
