What is the most informative bit in a noisy string?

You summarize a string of random bits with a single yes-or-no answer, while someone else sees only a noisy copy of the original string. Given a noisy copy of a string of bits, what's the best any single-bit statistic can do to tell us about the original string of bits? Turns out, you can't do better than just copying one of the bits.
